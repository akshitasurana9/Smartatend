About SmartAttend

SmartAttend is designed to simplify attendance management for educational institutions using biometric verification and digital records. Its goal is to make attendance workflows more organized, consistent and easier for authorized staff to review.

How It Works

A simple overview of the attendance workflow.

01

Select Class

The teacher selects the class and starts an attendance session.

02

Verify Identity

The system checks the student's identity through the configured verification method.

03

Validate Record

Identity and duplicate-entry checks are applied before saving attendance.

04

Save Attendance

Valid records can then be reviewed in attendance history and reports.
